# Sequence — Rules Reference

## Setup
- Two 52-card decks shuffled together (104 cards, no jokers). Each non-jack card
  appears on exactly two squares of a 10x10 board.
- Hand size by mode: 2 players = 7 cards each. 3 players or 4 players (2 teams of 2)
  = 6 cards each.

## A turn
1. Play a card from hand face-up on your discard pile.
2. Place one chip on either matching open square (if both are taken, see Dead Card).
3. Draw back up to your hand size.

## Jacks (not on the board)
- Two-eyed jack (wild): place a chip on any open square.
- One-eyed jack (anti-wild): remove one opponent chip from the board. Cannot remove a
  chip that is part of an already-completed sequence. Cannot place your own chip on
  the freshly emptied square in the same turn.

## Dead card
If both squares for a card in hand are already covered, discard it as dead, draw a
replacement, then take your normal turn.

## Corners and sequences
All 4 corners are free/wild for every player at once and count toward anyone's
sequence. A sequence through a corner needs only 4 chips, not 5. A sequence is 5
chips in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) with no gaps. One chip may be
shared between two sequences.

## Winning
- 2 players or 2 teams: need 2 sequences to win.
- 3 players or 3 teams: need only 1 sequence to win.

## Team play
Turn order alternates seats between teams, not by team block:
TeamA-P1 → TeamB-P1 → TeamA-P2 → TeamB-P2 → repeat.
A player never sees a teammate's hand.
